Still Have Questions? Let's Talk.
1. Who are your LinkedIn services for?
We work with founders, business owners, executives, and professionals who want their LinkedIn profile to reflect their expertise and attract the right opportunities.
2. Can you help me if I'm starting from zero?
Yes. Many of our clients begin with little or no LinkedIn activity. We start with your profile foundations, then build a content plan around your goals.
3. What exactly do you do?
We handle three things: strategy (finding your niche and direction), scripting (ghostwritten content in your voice), and management (profile optimisation and consistent posting).
4. Do I have to write my own LinkedIn posts?
No. Our voice ghostwriting team drafts posts based on your ideas and experience. You review and approve everything before it goes live.
5. How soon can I expect results?
Most clients notice changes in profile views and engagement within the first few weeks. Meaningful inbound opportunities typically build over 60 to 90 days of consistent work.
6. What is the ₹1 LinkedIn audit?
For ₹1 you get a preliminary profile gap analysis covering profile review, headline and About feedback, content direction, growth opportunities, and next-step recommendations. Additional services are explained separately before any purchase.
7. Will you create content that sounds like me?
That is the goal. We learn your tone, vocabulary, and point of view through interviews and past writing, so the content reads as genuinely yours.
8. Do I need to share my LinkedIn password?
No. We never need your password. Profile and content work is coordinated through the access and approvals you choose to give.
